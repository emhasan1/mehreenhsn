A few notes

Keep a little
room for wonder.

Not every idea needs a destination. Some just need a blank page, a cup of coffee, and the freedom to be a little strange.

Follow the detail that makes you look twice. The unexpected colour. The almost-right shape. The tiny moment that turns something useful into something memorable.

01
Note to self

Make it clear.
Then make it feel.

Start with what matters. Give it space. Let the rest fall away.

Leave something to discover: a small movement, a surprising interaction, a reason to stay for one more second.

More curiosity.
Less autopilot.
